# Difficulty: Easy | Clear textbook notes, neat handwriting

Classical mechanics describes the motion of macroscopic objects. Isaac Newton formulated three fundamental laws of motion that govern physical interactions.

Summary of Principles:
1. First Law: An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external net force.
2. Second Law: The rate of change of momentum is directly proportional to the applied force ($F = ma$).
3. Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

These principles form the foundation of civil engineering, aeronautics, and mechanical systems.
